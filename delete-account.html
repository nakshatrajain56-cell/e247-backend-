Account Deletion

Delete your 24/7 Education account

App: 24/7 Education (E247) — in.hustlecreates.e247
Developer: Nakshatra Jain, Ajmer, Rajasthan, India

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, directly inside the app. You do not need to contact us and you do not need to ask permission.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (instant)

  1. Open 24/7 Education.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are erased immediately.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

Email hustlecreates@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify the request and delete the account within 7 days, and always within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record
  • Your display name and friend code
  • Every study plan you created, and your membership of shared plans
  • All XP, ranks, streaks and progress
  • All of your notes, locked and unlocked, and your notes PIN
  • Any unused plan credits (these are forfeited and cannot be refunded)

This is permanent. Deleted data cannot be recovered by you or by us.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase records (date and amount of any in-app purchase, with no personal details) — retained for 8 years, because Indian tax law requires it. These cannot be linked back to you once your account is gone.
  • Short-lived abuse-prevention records — automatically expire, usually within 24 hours.

Nothing else is retained.
